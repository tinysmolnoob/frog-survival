FROG SURVIVAL

An open-world adventure where you are the frog
Explore the wild. Snakes, herons and hawks roam the land — if one spots you, a ! pops up and it comes for you.
Move: WASD / arrows  ·  Jump: Space  ·  Camera: drag mouse, scroll to zoom

Eat glowing 🪰 flies to keep your belly full.
